# 1. Introduction and Literature Survey (20%)

This section should include:

- Explanation of the **problem**
- **Project objectives**
- **Research hypothesis** (if applicable)
- In-depth review of **literature and related products**
- Discussion of **ethical considerations**

Ethical issues may include:

- Data protection
- Participant consent
- Equality, Diversity and Inclusion

After reading this section, we should clearly understand:

- What the project aims to do
- Why it is important

---

# 2. Design and Implementation (20%)

This section describes the **technical work**.

It should include:

- Description of **software lifecycle stages**
- Tools used in development
- Diagrams explaining design and implementation
- Code examples (especially difficult or revised parts)
- Explanation of **robustness and inclusivity considerations**

After reading this section, the reader should understand:

- What was built
- How it was implemented

---

# 3. Results and Evaluation (35%)

This section explains the **scientific investigation** of the project.

It should include:

- Testing procedures ensuring correct operation
- Performance evaluation against other work
- Statistical tests where appropriate
- Tables and figures presenting findings
- Critical evaluation of the results

We should clearly understand:

- How effectiveness was tested
- What the results show

---

# 4. Conclusions and Academic Quality (25%)

This final section should include:

- Evaluation of how well the **project aims were achieved**
- Implications of the findings
- Personal reflection on learning and performance
- Suggestions for **future work**

The overall report will also be assessed for:

- Structure and balance
- Quality of writing
- Clarity of figures, tables, diagrams
- Accuracy and completeness of references

After reading this section, we should understand:

- What was learned
- What future work could be done
